# 💧 M/S Shanti Enterprises – Smart RO Water Purifier Management System

A full-stack web application designed to modernize the management of an RO water purifier business. This project is being developed for **M/S Shanti Enterprises** to simplify customer service, technician management, product management, and business operations through a single digital platform.

---

## 📖 About the Project

The **Smart RO Water Purifier Management System** is a real-world business solution that enables customers to book RO services online, manage their RO machines, track service history, and receive maintenance reminders.

The system also provides dedicated dashboards for customers, administrators, and technicians, making business operations more organized and efficient.

This project is being built as a professional portfolio project while solving a real business problem.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Digitize RO water purifier business operations.
* Provide online service booking.
* Improve customer experience.
* Reduce manual paperwork.
* Manage technicians efficiently.
* Track products, services, and customers.
* Generate business reports and analytics.
* Build a scalable platform for future AI-powered features.

# 💡 Why This Project?

This project was inspired by a real business challenge.

Many local RO water purifier businesses still rely on phone calls, manual records, and paper-based service tracking. This platform aims to digitize these operations and improve customer satisfaction through online service management.
